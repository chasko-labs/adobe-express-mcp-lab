# spec — ASL avatars for firefly tic-tac-toe (bryanchasko.com/firefly-tic-tac-toe/asl-\*)

> **Status:** draft · **Type:** sibling sub-product (no extra S3 prefix) · **Routes:** `bryanchasko.com/firefly-tic-tac-toe/asl-demo` (Hugo page) + `bryanchasko.com/firefly-tic-tac-toe/asl-guide` (living guide, ever-improving)
> **Template:** `spec/use-cases/aws-builder-center-banner-spec.md` atomized pattern — IDs `ASL-*`
> **MCP:** `@adobe/express-developer-mcp@1.0.0` · `manifestVersion: 2` · `express-document-sdk` · iframe ↔ documentSandbox via `runtime.exposeApi`/`runtime.getApi`
> **Prior refs:** 1 asl-concept panel, 2 glimmer 72 glosses, 3 blender 42-joint rig, 4 contact-sheet/comfy/firefly, 5 mixer mask, 6 asl-lab 4 controls + 420ms, 7 firefly twin seed 4219

## objective

Ship ASL (American Sign Language) avatar integration for Firefly Tic-Tac-Toe as: (1) a **Hugo sibling demo page** at `/firefly-tic-tac-toe/asl-demo/` (`content/firefly-tic-tac-toe/asl-demo.md` + `layouts/page/asl-demo.html`) that previews the 6-frame gloss pipeline without Babylon on the marketing page; (2) an **ever-improving living guide** `docs/asl-concept-guide/README.md` mirrored to `/firefly-tic-tac-toe/asl-guide/` (same markdown, same `assets/frame-01.png`…`frame-06.png`); (3) this atomized spec (`ASL-*`) and companion doc `docs/firefly-tic-tac-toe/asl.md`. Hugo-managed sibling — no `s3-prefix-registry.json` mutation, no `buildspec-deploy.yml` exclude change (same root-bucket pattern as `FT-ROUTE-02` and `/sumerian-squares`).

## non-goals

- No change to `content/sumerian-squares/`, `assets/sumerian-squares/`, `layouts/page/sumerian-squares.html`.
- No WebGL/Babylon bundle on the Hugo guide/demo pages — Babylon lives in `addons/asl-concept` + `addons/asl-lab` and is documented, not rendered, in Hugo.
- No backend — Firefly calls from iframe only (`fetch` → `https://firefly-api.adobe.io/v2/images/generate`) proxied to sandbox; MSW mocks for tests.
- No new S3 bucket or prefix registry — Hugo root owns `/firefly-tic-tac-toe/` tree (FT-ROUTE-02 sibling rule carries over).
